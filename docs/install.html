Install the plugin

It is free. Download the installer for your computer, then follow the steps for it below. The first time, your computer will ask you to confirm that you trust it. That takes about a minute and you only do it once.

On a Mac

Apple shows a warning for any software that has not paid to be registered with Apple. The plugin is safe to install: it is made by the studio. These steps tell your Mac you trust it.

  1. Close your music software and download the installer

    Quit Logic Pro (or whatever you make music in). Press Download for Mac at the top of this page. The file lands in your Downloads folder and its name ends in .pkg.

  2. Double-click the file, then press Done

    Your Mac says it could not check the file. This is expected. Press Done. Do not move it to the Bin.

    Warning from your Mac

    "MusicallyUnorthodox-Mac.pkg" was not opened.

    Apple could not check it for software that might harm your Mac.

    Move to BinDone

  3. Open System Settings and choose Privacy & Security

    Click the Apple logo in the top left corner of the screen, then System Settings. In the list on the left, click Privacy & Security.

    System Settings
    General
    Appearance
    Privacy & Security
    Desktop & Dock

  4. Scroll to the bottom and press Open Anyway

    Scroll all the way down to the part called Security. You will see a line saying the installer was blocked. Press Open Anyway next to it.

    Privacy & Security, at the very bottom

    Security

    "MusicallyUnorthodox-Mac.pkg" was blocked to protect your Mac.

    Open Anyway

    Cannot see the button? Go back to step 2 and double-click the file again. The button only shows for a while after you have tried to open it.

  5. Confirm, and type your Mac password

    The warning comes back with a new button. Press Open Anyway, then type the password you use to log in to your Mac (or use Touch ID).

    Warning from your Mac

    Open "MusicallyUnorthodox-Mac.pkg"?

    Move to BinDoneOpen Anyway

  6. Click through the installer

    The installer opens. Press Continue, then Install, type your Mac password once more, and press Close when it says the installation was successful.

You only do this once. After that, the plugin tells you when there is a new version and updates itself from inside the plugin. No warning, no Settings.

On an older Mac (macOS 14 or earlier) there is a shortcut: hold Control, click the file, choose Open, then press Open.

On Windows

Windows shows a blue warning for software from a publisher it has not seen much of. The plugin is safe to install: it is made by the studio.

  1. Close your music software and download the installer

    Press Download for Windows at the top of this page. If your browser says the file is not commonly downloaded, choose Keep.

  2. Run the file and press More info

    Open your Downloads folder and double-click MusicallyUnorthodox-Windows-Setup. A blue window says Windows protected your PC. Click the words More info.

    Warning from Windows

    Windows protected your PC

    It stopped an app it does not recognise from starting. More info

    Don't run

  3. Press Run anyway

    A new button appears. Press Run anyway. If Windows asks whether to let the app make changes, press Yes.

    Warning from Windows

    Windows protected your PC

    App: MusicallyUnorthodox-Windows-Setup.exe

    Run anywayDon't run

  4. Click through the installer

    Follow the installer to the end and press Finish.

Updates on Windows. When the plugin says there is a new version, it downloads a new installer in your browser. Close your music software, run it, and follow steps 2 to 4 again.

Finding it in your music software

Open your music software again after installing. The plugin is called Musically Unorthodox.

  1. Logic Pro

    On a track, click an empty Audio FX slot, then Audio Units, then Musically Unorthodox. If it is not in the list, quit Logic completely and open it again.

  2. FL Studio, Ableton Live and others

    It installs as a VST3 plugin. Look under VST3 plugins for Musically Unorthodox. If it is not there, use your software's option to scan for new plugins.

  3. Put it on every track, and on the master

    Add it to each vocal and to the beat, and once more on the master (the stereo output). The copies talk to each other, which is how it balances and finishes the song.

Stuck on a step? Ask at the studio and bring your laptop.
